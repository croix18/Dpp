## One issue still awaiting Claude

**`MA.7.DP.1.1/chk/3` — Grouped homework-time histogram**

The student-facing question still says:

> “The histogram shows the numbers of minutes 25 students spent on homework last night. The teacher wants one number that, multiplied by 25, gives the total minutes the whole class spent on homework.”

**Editorial concern:** “Gives the total minutes” sounds like an **exact** calculation. However, the displayed histogram groups homework times into intervals, rather than listing every student's exact minutes. This is not an ordinary proofreading edit: the exactness of the claim is tied to the intended statistical reasoning. **Claude should determine the appropriate content-safe revision** (perhaps “estimate” rather than “gives,” or a changed premise). I have not changed the item or proposed answer choices.
