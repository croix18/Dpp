What is in this folder

ItemWhat it is
Unit foldersOne folder per Math Nation unit. Inside, one folder per benchmark, in teaching order. Each benchmark has three forms: 1 Check (5 questions, Thursday), 2 Re-check (3 questions, Wednesday after a reteach) and 3 Comparison (2 questions, Wednesday, for a benchmark that was not retaught).
In each form folderBuild sheet.pdf (title, description, every question's type, options and answer key), the question images Q1.png, Q2.png … to upload, Level scale.png for checks, and an Answers folder with the answer and worked solution for each question.

One-time setup: the template form (about 15 minutes)

  1. Upload this whole folder to your school Google Drive, so student responses stay in the school account.
  2. Make a new blank form named DPP template. Settings → Make this a quiz: on. Release grades: Immediately after each submission. Respondents can see: Missed questions and Point values on, Correct answers off.
  3. Settings → Responses: Collect email addresses: Verified, Limit to 1 response: on.
  4. Add these questions, all Required:
    1. Teacher: Multiple choice, Mr. C. Shaffer, Mr. J. Shaffer. 0 points.
    2. Period: Dropdown, your period numbers. 0 points.
    3. Choice question (copy me): Multiple choice with options A B C D. 1 point.
    4. Typed question (copy me): Short answer. ⋮ → Response validation → Regular expression → Matches → ^-?(\d+(\.\d+)?|\.\d+|\d+/\d+|\d+ \d+/\d+)$ with the error text Type only a number, like 12 or -3.5 or 2/7 or 4 1/3. No units, $, %, π or commas.. 1 point.
    5. Level (last): Multiple choice, Which level are you at right now? with options Level 1 to Level 4. 0 points.

Building one form (about 5 minutes for a check)

  1. In Drive, right-click DPP template → Make a copy. Rename it with the Form title from the build sheet, and paste the Description.
  2. For each question on the build sheet: click the copy me question of the right type, click Duplicate, drag it into place, click the image icon and pick Q1.png (Google Drive tab → this folder).
  3. Click Answer key: pick the letter, or for a typed question type every accepted answer from the build sheet and tick Mark all other answers incorrect. Checkbox questions (a few) are built from a plain Checkboxes question with the options on the sheet.
  4. Delete the two copy me questions. On a check, add Level scale.png to the Level question; on a re-check or comparison form, delete the Level question.
  5. Turn Locked mode on for no-calculator forms (the build sheet says which). Locked mode works only on school-managed Chromebooks, and it also blocks the Chromebook calculator.
  6. Click Preview, answer it once with the key to check the score is 5/5 (or 3/3, 2/2), then delete that test response.

The week with Forms

  1. Before Thursday: build or open the week's Check. Post its link in your Google Classroom and John's (as a co-teacher in his class, or send him the link to post).
  2. Thursday, last 8 minutes: students take the check. Forms scores it and shows each student their score; they choose their level at the end.
  3. Friday or Monday: open Responses → Summary. Frequently missed questions shows the item that gave the most trouble. That decides the reteach.
  1. Tuesday: reteach, 8–10 minutes.
  2. Wednesday: students take two short forms: the Re-check for the retaught benchmark and the Comparison form for the most recent benchmark that was not retaught.
  3. Data: Responses → Link to Sheets, and link every form to one spreadsheet. The Teacher and Period columns split the two classes.

Good to know

  • Typed answers. The validation only lets students submit a plain number, fraction or mixed number, so the accepted answers on each build sheet cover the ways a correct answer can be typed. If a student types a correct answer the key missed, open Responses → Question, find it, and mark it correct; Forms re-scores everyone who typed the same thing.
  • π answers. Students type only the number in front of π (for 7π they type 7). The question image says so.
  • Correct answers stay hidden until both teachers have given the form, so one class cannot pass answers to the next. Turn them on afterward if you want students to review.
  • What changed from the paper bank. Forms cannot grade a graph, an expression or a written explanation, so 59 of the 340 problems now ask students to choose (the right graph, expression, inequality or full answer) or check boxes; 4 were redrawn from Cadence. The build sheet marks each one, and the list is on the next page. Every other problem is unchanged from edition 4.1.
