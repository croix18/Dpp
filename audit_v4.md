# Item audit — edition 4.1 (Oct 10, 2026)

Edition 4 was built from the third Cadence revision. Three reviewers read every item and recomputed every key. The fixes below make edition 4.1, and **edition 4.1 is frozen**: a new Cadence no longer reshuffles the items.

## What round 4 found
- Of the 34 flags still open from edition 3, 14 are resolved and 20 persist (mostly variety and rigor).
- New flags: 5 high, 29 medium, 35 low. There was one key error: DP.2.4 Part C accepted answers the data rule out.
- The reviewers ran on a different model this round and were stricter: they rendered pages and checked the CPALMS clarifications. Part of the jump in medium and low flags comes from the reviewer, not the bank.

## Why the bank is frozen
Every Cadence revision redrew all 340 items. Each round fixed the old problems and drew new ones: 3 high flags in round 2, 3 in round 3, 5 in round 4. That pattern never reaches zero. Edition 4 is now saved item by item, and each problem is fixed in its own slot by an edit or a single redraw. The rest of the bank doesn't move, so a fix can't break anything else.

## Fixed in edition 4.1 (17 item slots)
- **The 5 high flags:**
  - GR.1.2 tent figure: redrawn so the bend at the 5 ft line shows.
  - DP.2.2 rigor: now asks for "the difference between the two probabilities" (it could have meant a ratio).
  - NSO.2.2 re-check: had no negative and no × or ÷. Replaced with −1.5 ÷ ¼.
  - DP.1.5 re-check: a second answer was defensible. That distractor is now a box plot.
  - AR.4.1 candle and plant situations: now name which two quantities are compared.
- **The key error:** DP.2.4 Part C accepts 2 only. Part B now says to use Part A.
- **AR.2.1:** a two-step distractor is replaced with a one-step one.
- **Nine re-check and comparison items reused a check item's numbers or setup:** GR.1.3, NSO.1.2, AR.3.2, GR.2.2, DP.1.5, AR.4.1 and AR.4.5 re-checks; AR.4.5 and AR.3.3 comparisons. These matter beyond tidiness. A student who remembers the check gets a head start on the re-check, which inflates the reteach gain your DPP evidence rests on. Each was redrawn with new numbers and a different item from the others on its strip.
- An independent reviewer checked the first 16 fixes and failed 4: a worked solution, a solution that misdescribed the data, an option that clashed with another item on the strip, and a Part B a student could answer two ways. All 4 are corrected. That reviewer also caught the AR.4.5 re-check and a rounding edge in AR.3.3; both are fixed.

## Layout fixes (mine)
- Figures on re-check and comparison pages printed at about a third of size, with labels at 2–4 pt. They now print at full size.
- Write-in items keep writing room: at least 0.6 in on every weekly check and 0.44 in on every strip. A strip that can't fit two copies with that room prints one copy per page and uses the whole page.
- Select-all letters sit before the box, and the key prints the scoring rule: 1 point only if every box is right.
- Scales fixed on the cover sheet:
  - AR.4.1 and AR.4.2 level 3 no longer promise "equation", which neither benchmark includes.
  - AR.3.3 level 3 lists area and drops time.

## Still worth reading before you copy
Nothing is on a must-read list this edition: all high flags and the key error are fixed. Keep the usual habit of reading a check once as you copy it.

## Left for Cadence (07 handoff)
About 35 medium and low flags remain, all rigor and variety:
- On about a dozen benchmarks the rigor item isn't the level-4 skill.
- Some benchmark verbs have no item: construct a circle graph, create a display, rhombus by diagonals.
- Some templates repeat across a benchmark's check, re-check and comparison.

None of these changes a student's score or the meaning of the data. They matter for next year's edition, which will be drawn fresh.
