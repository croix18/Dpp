Weekly check (5 items)
1. Regular A. 20.0 kilograms
   Each kilogram is about 2.2 pounds, so there are fewer kilograms than pounds: divide by 2.2.
   44 ÷ 2.2 ≈ 20.000, which rounds to 20.0 kilograms.

2. Regular 144.78 centimeters
   57 × 2.54 = 144.78 centimeters.

3. Regular 11 pounds
   5 × 2.2 = 11 pounds.

4. Regular 72.4 inches
   Each inch is 2.54 centimeters, so divide the centimeters by 2.54: 184 ÷ 2.54 ≈ 72.441.
   Rounded to the nearest tenth: 72.4 inches.
   Multiplying by 2.54 instead would give a number of inches larger than the number of centimeters, which cannot be right.

5. Rigor about 18.1 miles per hour
   In one hour (3600 seconds) it moves 8.1 × 3600 = 29160 meters, which is 29160 ÷ 1000 = 29.16 kilometers.
   Kilometers to miles: 29.16 ÷ 1.609 ≈ 18.123, which rounds to 18.1 miles per hour.
