Answer key — C-DP.2.1 · MA.7.DP.2.1 · Sample space of a simple experiment
Unit 2: Probability. Regular = on-benchmark item; Rigor = the item that needs the benchmark's reasoning. Mastery on the weekly
check = 4 of 5.

Weekly check (5 items)
1. Regular A. 6
   DOMAIN has 6 letters, so there are 6 cards, and no letter repeats.
   The outcome is the letter that is recorded, and a letter that is on more than one card is still one outcome.
   The sample space is {D, O, M , A, I, N }, which has 6 outcomes.

2. Regular {P , A, R, L, E}
   PARALLEL has 8 letters, so there are 8 cards, but A is on 2 cards and L is on 3 cards.
   The outcome is the letter, and a letter that is on more than one card is still one outcome, so each letter is listed once.
   The sample space is {P , A, R, L, E}.

3. Regular A. The sample space for the spinner has one outcome that the sample space for the bag does not have.
   Each outcome is a color, and a color is listed once no matter how many sections or marbles have it.
   Spinner: {orange, green, red, purple}. Bag: {orange, green, red}.
   The spinner can land on purple, and the bag has no purple marble, so the sample space for the spinner has one more outcome.

4. Regular {1, 5, 8, 9}
   The die has 8 faces, but some faces show the same number.
   Each different number is one outcome, so a number that is on more than one face is listed only once.
   The sample space is {1, 5, 8, 9}.

5. Rigor B. Spinning a spinner whose 6 equal sections are green, blue, purple, green, purple, blue; C. Drawing a
marble from a bag that holds 3 purple, 5 green and 4 blue marbles
   List the colors each experiment can give, each color once:
   Drawing a marble from a bag that holds 3 purple, 5 green and 4 blue marbles: {purple, green, blue}.
   Spinning a spinner whose 6 equal sections are green, blue, purple, green, purple, blue: {green, blue, purple}.
   Rolling a fair 6-sided die whose faces are blue, yellow, green, purple, green, purple: {blue, yellow, green, purple}.
   Drawing a marble from a bag that holds 3 purple, 5 green, 4 blue and 4 yellow marbles: {purple, green, blue, yellow}.
   Spinning a spinner whose 4 equal sections are green, purple, green, purple: {green, purple}.
   Only the experiments whose list is exactly {purple, green, blue} have this sample space; a color on several sections, faces or marbles is still one outcome.

Re-check strip (3 items, C-DP.2.1-R)
1. Regular A. {blue, red, purple, green}

2. Regular B. 5
   CIRCLE has 6 letters, so there are 6 cards, but C is on 2 cards.
   The outcome is the letter that is recorded, and a letter that is on more than one card is still one outcome.

   14 ÷ 7 = 2.

4. Regular C. 44

5. Rigor 28800 square feet
   Each inch on the drawing stands for 40 feet, so a square 1 inch on each side stands for a square 40 feet on each side: 40 × 40 = 1600 square feet.
