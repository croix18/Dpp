Answer key — C-AR.2.1 · MA.7.AR.2.1 · Solve one-step inequalities
Unit 10: Equations and Inequalities. Regular = on-benchmark item; Rigor = the item that needs the benchmark's
reasoning. Mastery on the weekly check = 4 of 5.
Weekly check (5 items)

1. Regular x ≤ 8
   "The product of a number x and 7" is 7x, and "at most 56" gives 7x ≤ 56.
   Divide both sides by 7. Because 7 is positive, the symbol does not change: x ≤ 8.

2. Regular x ≥ 7
   Subtract 4 from both sides.
   x ≥ 11 − 4 = 7


3. Regular x ≤ 5
   Divide both sides by 4.
   x≤5


4. Regular x ≥ 1
   Divide both sides by 5.
   x≥1


5. Rigor    A. Open circle at 3, shaded right
   Divide both sides by −4. Dividing by a negative reverses the symbol, so x > 3.
   An open circle at 3 shows that 3 is not included, shaded right.


Re-check strip (3 items, C-AR.2.1-R)
1. Regular x ≤ −4
   Divide both sides by 3.
   x ≤ −4


2. Regular x < 9
   Subtract 6 from both sides.
   x < 15 − 6 = 9


3. Regular x > 6
   "The product of a number x and 7" is 7x, and "greater than 42" gives 7x > 42.
   Divide both sides by 7. Because 7 is positive, the symbol does not change: x > 6.

Weekly check (5 items)

1. Regular    A straight line through the origin and (1, 3), (2, 6), (3, 9).
   The constant of proportionality 3 means y = 3x: each unit of hours gives 3 miles walked.
   Plot (0, 0), (1, 3), (2, 6) and draw the line through them.

2. Regular    A straight line through the origin and the points (1, 4), (2, 8) and (3, 12).
   In y = 4x the constant of proportionality is 4, so every point has y = 4 × x.
   Plot (0, 0), (1, 4), (2, 8), (3, 12) and draw the straight line through them.

3. Regular    The points (1, 5), (2, 10), (3, 15) and a straight line through them and the origin.
   Plot each row of the table as a point: (1, 5), (2, 10), (3, 15).
   A proportional relationship is a straight line through (0, 0), so extend the line through the origin.

4. Regular    Graph C
   A proportional relationship is a straight line through the origin.
   The constant of proportionality 1 is the slope: from (0, 0) the line rises 1 for each 1 unit to the right, passing through (1, 1).

5. Rigor     A straight line through (0, 0), (1, 8), (2, 16), (4, 32); when x = 3, y = 24.
   Plot the three table points and draw the straight line through them and the origin.
   The line passes through (3, 24) — the constant of proportionality is 8, so y = 8 × 3 = 24.

Answer key — C-AR.4.2 · MA.7.AR.4.2 · Constant of proportionality
Unit 11: Representing Proportional Relationships. Regular = on-benchmark item; Rigor = the item that needs the
benchmark's reasoning. Mastery on the weekly check = 4 of 5.
Weekly check (5 items)

1. Regular    A. 0.5
   The constant of proportionality is liters of paint per wall: 1.5 ÷ 3 = 0.5.

2. Regular    25
   The constant of proportionality is the unit rate: 125 ÷ 5 = 25.

3. Regular    6
   The constant of proportionality is y ÷ x.
   12 ÷ 2 = 6, and every pair gives the same ratio.


4. Regular    1.75
   The constant of proportionality is the fee divided by the length.
   24.5 ÷ 14 = 1.75, and 28 ÷ 16 = 1.75.
   The constant of proportionality is 1.75 dollars per foot.

5. Rigor     B. the amount earned for each hour worked
   The constant of proportionality is the unit rate — how much of the first quantity goes with one of the second.
   Here that is the amount earned for each hour worked.


Re-check strip (3 items, C-AR.4.2-R)
