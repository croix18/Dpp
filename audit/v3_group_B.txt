1. Regular C. 18.2 kilograms

   40 ÷ 2.2 ≈ 18.182, which rounds to 18.2 kilograms.

2. Regular 58.75 Swiss francs
   Each dollar becomes 0.9 Swiss francs: 100 × 0.9 = 90.00 Swiss francs.
   Subtract the meal: 90.00 − 31.25 = 58.75 Swiss francs.

3. Regular 77.2 inches
   Each inch is 2.54 centimeters, so divide the centimeters by 2.54: 196 ÷ 2.54 ≈ 77.165.
   Rounded to the nearest tenth: 77.2 inches.
   Multiplying by 2.54 instead would give a number of inches larger than the number of centimeters, which cannot be right.

4. Regular $59.80
   Convert minutes to hours: 195 ÷ 60 = 3.25 hours.
   Pay: 3.25 × $18.40 = $59.80.

earns 0.
