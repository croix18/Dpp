Unit 2: Probability. Regular = on-benchmark item; Rigor = the item that needs the benchmark's reasoning. Mastery on
the weekly check = 4 of 5.
Weekly check (5 items)

1. Regular {G, R, A, P , H}
   Each card that can be drawn is one outcome, so the sample space lists every card in the bag.
   GRAPH has 5 different letters, so there are 5 cards and 5 outcomes: {G, R, A, P , H}.

2. Regular    A. 4
   Each section is one possible outcome, and the spinner is spun once.
   The sample space has 4 outcomes: yellow, white, red, orange.

3. Regular    B. 3
   The outcome recorded is the color, so the sample space is {green, red, black} — 3 outcomes.
   The 13 marbles are not themselves the outcomes.

4. Regular    B. 32
   Every card in the deck is one possible outcome of a single draw.
   The deck has 4 × 8 = 32 cards, so the sample space has 32 outcomes.

5. Rigor     C. 3
   The color is what gets recorded, so the outcomes are yellow, purple, green — 3 outcomes.
   The 7 sections are not all different outcomes, because a color can appear on more than one section.
