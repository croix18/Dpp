Answer key — C-AR.4.1 · MA.7.AR.4.1 · Is it proportional? (table, graph, description)
Unit 11: Representing Proportional Relationships. Regular = on-benchmark item; Rigor = the item that needs the benchmark's
reasoning. Mastery on the weekly check = 4 of 5.

Weekly check (5 items)
1. Regular A. A printer prints 30 pages every minute.; E. A store sells oranges at $2.40 per pound.
   A relationship is proportional only when the two quantities have a constant ratio and both start at 0.
   Proportional: "A store sells oranges at $2.40 per pound." — the same price for every pound, and 0 pounds cost $0; "A printer prints 30 pages every minute." — the
   same pages every minute, and 0 minutes print 0 pages.
   Not proportional: "A phone plan costs $36 plus $0.10 for each text message." — the $36 is charged even with 0 texts; "A candle is 12 inches tall and burns down 1
   inch each hour." — the candle is 12 inches tall at hour 0; "A taxi charges $3.00 plus $2.00 per mile." — the $3.00 charge is paid before the first mile.

2. Regular C. No, because y ÷ x is not the same for every pair of values.
   Check y ÷ x for each pair: 5 ÷ 1 = 5, 10 ÷ 2 = 5, 23 ÷ 4 = 5.75, 25 ÷ 5 = 5.
   The ratios are not all the same, so the relationship is not proportional.

3. Regular A. Yes, because the line is straight and passes through the origin.
   A graph shows a proportional relationship when it is a straight line through the origin (0, 0).
   This line passes through (0, 0), so it is proportional.

4. Regular C. No, because there is a setup time of 15 minutes before the first box packed.
   A proportional relationship must pass through (0, 0) and have a constant ratio.
   The starting setup time of 15 means 0 boxes packed already gives 15 minutes, so it is not proportional.

5. Rigor Noor's reason is not correct; the relationship is proportional, with constant of proportionality 1.2. Score: 1
point for all three parts (the reason judged, the proportional decision, and the constant or a pair with a
different ratio, in any equivalent form); two parts earn 0.
   A table does not have to show (0, 0); test the ratio y ÷ x for every pair.
   1.8 ÷ 1.5 = 1.2, 2.4 ÷ 2 = 1.2, 3 ÷ 2.5 = 1.2, 6 ÷ 5 = 1.2.
   Every ratio is 1.2, so the relationship is proportional and the constant of proportionality is 1.2.

   The gardens are the same size, and far more values of Garden A lie near 132, so the sunflower is more likely from Garden A.

2. Regular B. Group B typically exercised about 10 more minutes per day than Group A.
   Group A has a median of 35 minutes and Group B has a median of 45 minutes.
   45 − 35 = 10, so: Group B typically exercised about 10 more minutes per day than Group A.
