   The sample space is {red circle, yellow star, yellow triangle}.

3. Regular A. The sample space for the spinner has one outcome that the sample space for the bag does not have.

   The spinner can land on purple, and the bag has no purple marble, so the sample space for the spinner has one more outcome.

4. Regular {1, 5, 8, 9}

   With the π key: 106.81 ÷ π ≈ 34.00 feet.

   2 × 3.14 = 6.28, and 62.80 ÷ 6.28 = 10.00 centimeters.

4. Regular 12.57 feet
   C = 2πr = 2π(2) = 4π .
   With the π key: 4 × π ≈ 12.57 feet.

5. Rigor 63 complete turns
   In one turn, the wheel rolls a distance equal to its circumference: C = πd = 3.14 × 12 = 37.68 inches.
   200 feet = 200 × 12 = 2400 inches.
   2400 ÷ 37.68 ≈ 63.69 turns.
   The wheel makes 63 complete turns; the last turn is not finished.

3. Regular 6
