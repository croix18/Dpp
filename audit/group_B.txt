Answer key — C-AR.3.2 · MA.7.AR.3.2 · Solve problems involving proportions
Unit 6: Multi-Step Problems With Proportional Relationships. Regular = on-benchmark item; Rigor = the item that needs
the benchmark's reasoning. Mastery on the weekly check = 4 of 5.
Weekly check (5 items)

1. Regular       B. 52 km
   Scale: 16 ÷ 4 = 4 km for every 1 cm.
   13 × 4 = 52 km.


2. Regular       C. 12 ft
   The tree's shadow is 18 ÷ 6 = 3 times as long as the person's.
   The tree is 3 times as tall: 4 × 3 = 12 ft.

3. Regular       75 pounds
   Unit rate: 25 ÷ 5 = 5 lb/bag
   15 × 5 = 75 pounds


4. Regular       $30.72
   The cost is proportional to the weight, so find the cost of one pound.
   $5.76 ÷ 1.5 = $3.84 for one pound.
   $3.84 × 8 = $30.72.


5. Rigor     7 hours 30 minutes
   The rate is constant, so scale both quantities by the same factor: 360 ÷ 12 = 30.
   The time is 30 × 15 = 450 minutes.
   450 minutes is 7 × 60 = 420 minutes plus 30 minutes, so the time is 7 hours 30 minutes.


Re-check strip (3 items, C-AR.3.2-R)

1. Regular       C. 60 km
   Scale: 12 ÷ 3 = 4 km for every 1 cm.
   15 × 4 = 60 km.

   The tree is 3 times as tall: 4 × 3 = 12 ft.

3. Regular       6 cups of mix

Answer key — C-AR.4.5 · MA.7.AR.4.5 · Real-world proportional problems
Unit 6: Multi-Step Problems With Proportional Relationships. Regular = on-benchmark item; Rigor = the item that needs
the benchmark's reasoning. Mastery on the weekly check = 4 of 5.
Weekly check (5 items)

1. Regular    $132.00
   $22.00 × 6 = $132.00.


2. Regular    54 pages
   Unit rate: 15 ÷ 5 = 3 pages per minute.
   3 × 18 = 54 pages.


3. Regular    B. 24 gallons
   Unit rate: 256 ÷ 8 = 32 miles per gallon.
   768 ÷ 32 = 24 gallons.


4. Regular    8 cups
   36 ÷ 9 = 4, so the recipe scales up 4 times.
   2 × 4 = 8 cups.


5. Rigor     5 times
   One full tank travels 19.6 × 16.3 = 319.48 miles.
   1650 ÷ 319.48 ≈ 5.16, so the trip needs 6 tankfuls of gas.
   The first tankful is already in the car, so the driver must fill the tank 5 times.


Re-check strip (3 items, C-AR.4.5-R)

1. Regular    D. 16 gallons
   Unit rate: 200 ÷ 8 = 25 miles per gallon.
   400 ÷ 25 = 16 gallons.


2. Regular    12 cups
   36 ÷ 9 = 4, so the recipe scales up 4 times.
   3 × 4 = 12 cups.


3. Regular    72 pages
   Unit rate: 32 ÷ 4 = 8 pages per minute.
   8 × 9 = 72 pages.

Unit 9: Algebraic Expressions. Regular = on-benchmark item; Rigor = the item that needs the benchmark's reasoning.

Weekly check (5 items)

1. Regular    D. 4(4x + 8)
   Both terms of 16x + 32 are divisible by 4.
   16x ÷ 4 = 4x and 32 ÷ 4 = 8, so 16x + 32 = 4(4x + 8).


2. Regular    A. 5(2x) + 5(3); E. 10x + 15
   Distribute 5 to both terms: 5 × 2x = 10x and 5 × 3 = 15.
   So 5(2x + 3) = 10x + 15, which is also written 5(2x) + 5(3).

3. Regular    C. 8x + 20
   The student added 8x and 20 as if they were like terms.
   An x-term and a constant cannot be combined: 4(2x + 5) = 8x + 20.

4. Regular    C. 30x + 5
   Distribute 5 to both terms inside the parentheses.
   5 × 6x = 30x and 5 × 1 = 5, so the result is 30x + 5.


5. Rigor     B. No, because the first simplifies to 5x − 1, not 5x + 1.
   Distribute and combine: 2(2x − 4) = 4x − 8, then + x + 7 gives 5x − 1.
   The second expression is 5x + 1.
   The x-terms match but the constants differ (−1 vs 1), so they are not equivalent.

1. Regular    A. 4x + 12; B. 2(2x) + 2(6)
